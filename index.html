THE NUN (2018)



In a remote Romanian abbey, a nun is found dead under mysterious circumstances, prompting the Vatican to send Father Burke and Sister Irene to investigate, with the help of a local villager named Frenchie. Inside the abbey, they uncover that an ancient demon called Valak has been unleashed and is terrorizing anyone who enters. After facing a series of terrifying supernatural events, they manage to seal Valak again using a relic containing the blood of Jesus though subtle signs hint that the evil hasn’t been completely destroyed.

THE NUN II (2023)

Five years after the events in Romania, Sister Irene is drawn back into danger when a chain of brutal deaths across Europe points to the resurgence of Valak. Meanwhile, Frenchie now working at an old boarding school in France unknowingly serves as Valak’s host, as the demon slowly tries to possess him fully. Irene must confront Valak once more, racing against time to save Frenchie and stop the demon before its power becomes unstoppable
